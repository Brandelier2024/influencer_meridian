1. Organiser

The Meridian Brand Ambassador Contest (“Contest”) is organised and conducted by Meridian Ice Cream (Chavan & Sons), having its registered office at S. No. 25/6/18, Nanded, Nanded Shivane Link Road, Opp. Shravani Heights, Nanded Phata, Pune – 411041, Maharashtra, India, hereinafter referred to as “Meridian” or the “Organiser”.

These Terms and Conditions govern participation in the Contest. Submission of an entry shall constitute acceptance of these Terms and Conditions by the participant and, where applicable, their parent or legal guardian.

2. Contest Period

The timeline for the Contest is established as follows:

  • Entry Commencement Date: 18 November 2026
  • Entry Closing Date: 15 February 2027
  • Winner Announcement Date: 5 March 2027

All dates and timings shall be interpreted according to Indian Standard Time (IST).

Meridian reserves the right to extend the entry period by announcing the revised deadline through its official communication channels.

3. Eligibility Criteria

3.1. The Contest shall be open to individuals of all age groups residing in India.

3.2. Participants must maintain a genuine and active social-media account. Participants below the minimum age permitted by the relevant social-media platform must participate through an account managed by their parent or legal guardian, in accordance with the platform's applicable policies.

3.3. Participants below 18 years of age must obtain verifiable consent from their parent or legal guardian before participating. The parent or legal guardian shall be responsible for providing the required permissions and accepting the applicable terms on behalf of the minor.

3.4. Participants must not have any existing endorsement, sponsorship or professional association with a competing ice-cream, frozen-dessert or directly competing dessert brand during the Contest period.

3.5. Employees, directors, management personnel, agencies, vendors and individuals directly involved in organising or evaluating the Contest, together with their immediate family members, shall not be eligible to participate.

4. How to Participate

To enter the Contest, participants must complete the following steps:

  1. Create an original video or Instagram Reel of 30–45 seconds featuring Meridian Ice Cream or expressing their genuine association with, appreciation for, or enthusiasm towards the brand.
  2. Include an original dialogue, voice-over, jingle, tagline, performance or other creative concept. Participants may also create an original brand reel using Meridian's official music/jingle.
  3. Follow Meridian's official Instagram account: @meridianicecream.
  4. Tag Meridian's official account in the submitted Reel.
  5. Use the official Contest hashtags: #MeridianIceCream #MeridianBrandAmbassador #VoiceOfMeridian #TasteTheRealFruit.
  6. Keep the submitted Reel publicly accessible on their social-media profile until the final results are announced.
  7. Complete the official Contest registration form available at the official portal (influencer.meridianicecreams.com/#register).
  8. Submit the entry within the stipulated Contest period.

4.1. Participants may submit unlimited entries during the Contest period, provided each entry is original and independently created.

4.2. Each entry shall be evaluated separately. Submission of multiple entries does not guarantee additional consideration, shortlisting or selection.

4.3. Entries submitted through any method other than the officially prescribed process may be rejected.

4.4. 100% Free Entry: Participation shall be entirely voluntary. No purchase of Meridian products or payment of any participation fee shall be required.

4.5. Submission of an entry does not guarantee shortlisting, selection or appointment as a Meridian Brand Ambassador.

5. Contest Categories

5.1. Meridian conducts the Contest across 5 distinct creative categories:

  • Male Solo Creator: Individual male creators showcasing creative storytelling, screen presence, and brand love.
  • Female Solo Creator: Individual female creators expressing their passion for Meridian through creative vibe and charisma.
  • Kids Category: Young creators and children showcasing wholesome childhood memories and authentic delight (under parental supervision).
  • Group / Family Category: Family circles, friends, and teams capturing shared celebrations and ice cream bonding moments.
  • Voice of Meridian: Vocalists, voice-over artists, rappers, and creators delivering original brand jingles, taglines, dialogues, or musical tributes.

5.2. The applicable categories, number of winners and category-specific eligibility requirements shall be announced through Meridian's official communication channels.

5.3. Meridian reserves the right to determine the allocation of participants to the appropriate categories based on the information submitted during registration.

5.4. Meridian may introduce, modify, combine or discontinue categories before the final evaluation, provided any material changes are communicated through its official channels and are not made arbitrarily to disadvantage participants.

5.5. The selection of winners shall be category-wise, based on the applicable evaluation criteria.

6. Content and Creative Guidelines

All submitted content must:

  • a. Be original and independently created by the participant.
  • b. Feature only individuals who have provided appropriate consent to appear in the content.
  • c. Use only music, photographs, videos, graphics and other creative materials for which the participant possesses the necessary rights or permissions.
  • d. Represent Meridian Ice Cream, its products and brand identity accurately and respectfully.
  • e. Be appropriate for a family audience.
  • f. Comply with applicable Indian laws and the terms and policies of the relevant social-media platform.
Strictly Prohibited Content: The following content shall not be permitted and will result in immediate rejection:
  • Obscene, abusive, discriminatory, hateful, defamatory or sexually explicit material.
  • Misleading product claims or unsubstantiated health, nutritional or promotional claims.
  • Content promoting unsafe activities or unlawful conduct.
  • Unauthorised use of copyrighted music, images, videos, trademarks or other intellectual property.
  • Content that deliberately damages, misrepresents or disparages Meridian or its products.
  • Content promoting competing brands or products.
  • Artificially generated or manipulated content intended to impersonate another individual without authorisation.

Meridian reserves the right to reject, remove or disqualify any entry that violates these guidelines.

8. Evaluation and Selection Process

8.1. Eligible entries shall be evaluated by an expert selection panel appointed by Meridian.

8.2. The selection process comprises two integral pillars: Jury Evaluation and Social-Media Engagement, evaluated under the following structure:

Pillar Evaluation Parameters
Pillar 1: Jury Evaluation Panel
Screen Presence Personality, charisma, and camera confidence
Creativity & Originality Concept uniqueness, visual storytelling, and innovative approach
Communication & Clarity Diction, vocal articulation, expression, and audio balance
Brand Affinity Authentic enthusiasm and genuine connection with Meridian Ice Cream
Family Suitability Wholesome, family-friendly brand tone aligned with values
Production Quality Framing, lighting, editing finesse, and overall aesthetic delivery
Pillar 2: Social-Media Engagement
Audience Resonance Verified organic likes, genuine comments, and authentic shares

8.3. Social-media engagement shall be assessed using eligible and verifiable engagement received during the officially specified evaluation period.

8.4. Meridian may establish and announce a standard engagement calculation methodology to ensure fair comparison between entries.

8.5. Anti-Fraud & Anti-Bot Clause: Purchased followers, automated engagement, fake likes, fraudulent comments, artificial sharing or any other manipulation of engagement shall not be considered valid and will trigger disqualification.

8.6. Likes, comments, shares and follower count shall not independently determine the winner.

8.7. Meridian may conduct additional auditions, interviews, presentations or practical creative assignments for shortlisted participants.

8.8. The selection panel's decision shall be final and binding in respect of the evaluation of entries, subject to applicable law and correction of demonstrable errors, fraud or procedural irregularities.

8.9. Meridian shall not be obligated to disclose confidential internal deliberations, individual judges' comments or the detailed scores of other participants.

8.10. In case of a tie, Meridian shall have the right to conduct an additional evaluation based on creativity, originality and overall suitability for the relevant category.

9.2. Meridian reserves the right to determine the number of winners, category-wise prize allocation and distribution of product vouchers. The applicable prize details and redemption conditions shall be communicated before the Contest closes.

9.3. The title shall not automatically establish any employment, agency, partnership, franchise, joint venture or permanent contractual relationship between Meridian and the selected participant.

9.4. Any professional assignment, paid promotional activity, appearance, shoot or endorsement obligation shall be governed by a separate written Brand Ambassador Agreement.

9.5. Meridian shall not be obligated to provide any minimum number of assignments, shoots, paid engagements or promotional opportunities unless expressly agreed in writing.

9.6. Prizes shall not be transferable, exchangeable for cash or substituted at the participant's request, except where required by law or expressly approved by Meridian.

9.7. Applicable taxes, if any, shall be dealt with in accordance with prevailing Indian tax laws.

12. Brand Ambassador Conduct and Exclusivity

12.1. During the 12-month ambassador appointment, the selected participant shall maintain professional conduct and shall not knowingly engage in activities that materially harm or bring Meridian's brand or reputation into disrepute.

12.2. Exclusivity Clause: The selected ambassador shall not endorse, promote or enter into a commercial association with a directly competing ice-cream or frozen-dessert brand during the appointment period without Meridian's prior written approval.

12.3. The ambassador shall not make unauthorised statements, product claims, pricing commitments or promotional announcements on behalf of Meridian.

12.4. Any use of Meridian's name, logo, trademarks or official communication materials must be authorised by Meridian.

12.5. Detailed deliverables, compensation, exclusivity, notice period, termination rights and other engagement conditions shall be governed by the separate Brand Ambassador Agreement.

12.6. Where the selected ambassador is below 18 years of age, all promotional assignments and related agreements shall be subject to appropriate parental or legal guardian consent and applicable child-protection and advertising requirements.
