2. Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to all individuals who register, submit content, or interact with the Meridian Brand Ambassador Contest via our official web portal (influencer.meridianicecreams.com) or our official social media touchpoints.

This policy complements and operates in direct conjunction with Section 13 (Personal Information and Privacy) of the Official Contest Terms & Conditions.

4. Lawful Purpose and Grounds of Processing

In accordance with the DPDP Act 2023, Meridian processes your personal data on the lawful basis of your express and informed consent granted during registration:

  • Administering Participation: Validating entry details and assigning entries to one of the 5 contest categories (Male, Female, Kids, Group/Family, Voice of Meridian).
  • Evaluation by Selection Panel: Enabling the expert jury to assess video submissions against official criteria.
  • Social Media Engagement Verification: Auditing engagement to ensure compliance with our anti-bot and anti-fraud guidelines.
  • Winner Notification: Contacting finalists and winners via official phone, email, and Instagram channels within the 72-hour notification window.
  • Brand Ambassador Contracting: Preparing and executing the formal 12-month honorary Brand Ambassador appointment agreements and voucher issuance.
  • Statutory & Tax Compliance: Maintaining accounting and tax compliance records for any gifted prizes or vouchers under Indian tax law.

5. Special Protection for Children & Minors (Under 18)

Parental Consent Mandate: Meridian enforces strict safeguards for participants under the age of 18, in full compliance with the DPDP Act 2023.

5.1. Verifiable Guardian Consent: Any participant below 18 years of age must participate only with the verifiable permission of a parent or legal guardian. The parent or guardian must review and approve these Terms on the minor's behalf.

5.2. Prohibition of Tracking & Profiling: Meridian explicitly does not undertake any tracking, behavioral monitoring, or targeted commercial advertising directed towards children.

5.3. Parental Communication: All notifications, verifications, and contractual arrangements concerning a minor winner are conducted directly with their verified parent or legal guardian.

6. Strict Non-Sale & Zero Third-Party Sharing

Absolute Non-Sale Guarantee: Meridian Ice Cream will NEVER sell, lease, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to third-party marketing companies, data brokers, or advertisement networks under any circumstances.

Your data is accessed only by authorized personnel on a strict need-to-know basis:

  • Jury Panel: Appointed evaluation judges reviewing submitted video links and creative entries.
  • Technical Infrastructure Partners: Secure cloud and database hosting providers (e.g., Hostinger) bound by strict non-disclosure and security covenants.
  • Statutory Authorities: Disclosed only if formally required by Indian law enforcement, court subpoenas, or regulatory authorities.

7. Data Security and Technical Safeguards

Meridian implements enterprise-grade technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, accidental alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

  • Encrypted Transport: All data transmitted between your browser and our server is secured using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption.
  • Secure Relational Database: Registrations are stored in a hardened MySQL database utilizing prepared statements to eliminate SQL injection risks.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Administrative access to contestant data is restricted strictly to authorized superadmin personnel with multi-factor authentication, separate from the public website.
  • Rate Limiting & Anti-Brute-Force: Submission endpoints feature IP-based rate limiting to prevent automated spam and DDoS vectors.

8. Data Retention Schedule

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected:

  • Active Contest Entries: Maintained during the active contest and evaluation timeline (18 November 2026 to 5 March 2027) plus a standard 90-day dispute resolution buffer.
  • Brand Ambassador Records: For appointed winners, records and agreements are retained for the duration of the 12-month ambassadorship plus any statutory tax audit period required under Indian law.
  • Secure Erasure: Following the expiration of the retention window, contestant personal records are securely purged or anonymized.

9. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you (or your parent/guardian, if under 18) possess the following statutory rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request a summary of the personal data Meridian holds concerning your contest entry.
  • Right to Correction / Rectification: If your submitted phone number, email, or details contain typographical errors, you may request correction.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time by contacting our Privacy Desk. (Please note that withdrawing consent prior to winner selection terminates active eligibility in the contest.)
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You have the right to register complaints or seek resolution regarding the handling of your data through our designated Grievance Officer.
